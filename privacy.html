Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

Zhumeng Workshop ("we", "us", or "our") is an early-stage mobile game publishing studio. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our mobile games and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, language, and screen resolution.
  • Advertising identifiers: such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), used for advertising and measurement (you may reset or limit this in your device settings).
  • App usage data: crash logs, performance data, and game progress (if local or cloud save is used).
  • Network information: IP address (processed by advertising and analytics providers).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve our games and features;
  • To show ads through third-party advertising SDKs such as AppLovin MAX;
  • To analyze app performance and fix crashes;
  • To comply with applicable laws and regulations.

3. Third-Party Services

Our apps may integrate third-party SDKs, including:

  • AppLovin MAX — advertising and monetization. See the AppLovin Privacy Policy.
  • Other advertising or analytics networks (we will update this page if additional partners are added).

These third parties may collect and process data independently under their own privacy policies.

4. Children's Privacy

Our games are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us so we can delete it.

5. Data Retention and Security

We retain information only for as long as needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

6. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Users in the EU/UK may exercise rights under the GDPR. You can also reset your advertising ID or limit personalized ads in your device settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated date will be posted on this page. Continued use of our apps after changes means you accept the updated policy.
