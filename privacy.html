This Privacy Policy explains how Zhumeng Workshop ("we," "our," or "the Developer") handles your information when you use our mobile applications, including "Repo Game: Haul N Escape" (each, an "App").

1. Data We Collect During Use

When you download, access, and interact with an App, we and our service providers may automatically log certain technical and behavioural information. This data helps the App operate, supports performance monitoring, advertising, and service improvement. The information may include:

  • The Internet Protocol (IP) address assigned to your device, used for network communication and basic diagnostics.
  • Records of in-App activity, including features you use, interaction timestamps, and session duration.

  • Operating system version and hardware model of your mobile device.
  • Advertising identifiers, such as the Google Advertising ID (GAID), used for advertising and measurement. You may reset or limit this in your device settings.

Our Apps do not request, track, or store your device's precise geographic location (such as GPS coordinates).

If you voluntarily supply your email address or other contact details (for example, by reaching out for support), we may use that information to respond to your enquiries or send important service notifications. Any personal data you provide will be used only for the purpose you intended.
